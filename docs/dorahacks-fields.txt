=== Project Description (max 960 chars) ===

19,000+ AI agents on ERC-8004 hire and pay each other, yet nothing a contract can check says which deserve trust. Read raw it misleads: 346 of 367 rated agents had one reviewer, one wallet wrote 225 reviews, and clone-scale owners made 83% of recent registrations.

Tinjau is a verified background check before money moves. Its Creditcoin contract admits data only through Attestcoin proofs of Ethereum transactions, so a fact enters only after the precompile verified it. No score: consumers pass their own thresholds, get admitted evidence plus known gaps, and can replay every proof.

An escrow prices a hire and refuses records with holes; a bounty pays for evidence that changes a decision, either way. A scout proves both ways, and a dishonest one can only withhold, which shows up as a gap.

Live on CC3 testnet: 33 mainnet transactions admitted back to 2022, two hired at 1%, one gated, the same proof verified on CC3 mainnet.


=== USC Integration Summary (max 960 chars) ===

Every fact enters through USC. GroundedFacts has no admin, no setter and no trusted relayer: the only way to change its state is `record`, and every proof in that call goes through BlockProver first.

BlockProver 0x..0FD2 `verify` runs on each proof before a byte of it is read; a failed proof reverts. `calculateTxIndex` keys each transaction by (chainKey, height, txIndex), so duplicates are skipped and ownership transfers order exactly. AttestorStash 0x..0FD4 reports the attestor count at admission, so the escrow can refuse a thin set. ChainInfo 0x..0FD3 gives the attested tip, so a hirer can refuse stale facts.

Without USC this cannot exist: every fact comes from Ethereum history, which no EVM contract can read, least of all from another chain. `record` would have to trust its caller.

Gas on `verify` tracks continuity roots: 62,292 at 7 roots up to 631,434 at 984. The same proof returns true on CC3 mainnet at identical gas.
