=== Project Description ===

19,000+ AI agents are registered on ERC-8004 on Ethereum mainnet and already pay
each other, yet nothing a contract can check says which deserve trust. Read raw,
the registry misleads: 346 of 367 rated agents had one reviewer, one wallet wrote
225 reviews, and clone-scale owners made 83% of recent registrations.

Tinjau is a verified background check before money moves, a bureau in
Creditcoin's sense: it records and does not judge. GroundedFacts admits data only
through Attestcoin proofs of Ethereum transactions, so a fact enters only after
the precompile verified it. There is no score. Consumers pass their own
thresholds and get admitted evidence plus known gaps, and anyone can replay every
proof from chain data.

AgentHireEscrow prices a hire from those facts and refuses records with holes.
CoverageBounty pays for evidence that changes a decision, in either direction. An
autonomous scout proves evidence both ways; a dishonest one can only withhold,
which the registry's own counter exposes as a gap.

Live on CC3 testnet: 33 Ethereum mainnet transactions admitted, back to 2022 and
still growing; two agents hired at 1%; one gated; a bounty claimed by the scout;
the same proof verified on CC3 mainnet.

Limits: Ethereum mainnet only; admitted evidence with known gaps, never a
complete history.


=== USC Integration Summary ===

Every fact in Tinjau enters through USC. GroundedFacts has no admin, no setter
and no trusted relayer: the only way to change its state is `record`, and every
proof in that call goes through BlockProver first.

Precompiles, called inside the contract:

- BlockProver 0x...0FD2 `verify`: run on each proof before a byte of it is read.
  A failed proof reverts with ProofRejected(i).
- BlockProver `calculateTxIndex`: keys each transaction by (chainKey, height,
  txIndex), so duplicates are skipped and several scouts can race safely. It also
  orders ownership transfers exactly, so the newest proven transfer wins whatever
  order the proofs arrive in.
- AttestorStash 0x...0FD4 `getAttestorsCount`: read on every admitted proof.
  facts().minAttestors is the lowest attestor count registered when any of the
  agent's facts was admitted, and the escrow can refuse a thin set (ThinQuorum).
  Live: 4 attestors for Ethereum mainnet, 100 CTC bond each.
- ChainInfo 0x...0FD3: attestedTip() shows how far Creditcoin has attested, so
  with facts().coveredThrough a hirer can refuse stale facts (Stale).

The official EvmV1Decoder from @gluwa/usc-contracts decodes the proven bytes.
Logs of a reverted source transaction are never used, and a log counts only when
its emitter is the ERC-8004 registry configured for that chainKey, so a
look-alike event from any other contract is ignored.

Without USC this product cannot exist. Every fact comes from Ethereum history,
which no EVM contract can read, least of all from another chain. `record` would
have to trust its caller, and Tinjau would be one more signed off-chain
aggregator, which is exactly what ERC-8004 leaves consumers to trust today. With
USC the contract checks the history itself and can lock money on the result.

Gas on `verify` tracks continuity roots rather than age: 62,292 at 7 roots up to
631,434 at 984. The same proof returns verify = true on CC3 mainnet at the same
127,746 gas, so only the chainKey changes. recomputeFromChain() replayed all 33
admitted transactions on 13 Sep 2026 and produced facts identical to facts(); the
MCP tool tinjau_verify exposes that check to any agent.

USC moves trust from RPCs and indexers to Creditcoin's bonded attestors; it does
not remove it. minAttestors is the count registered at admission, not proof
signers.
