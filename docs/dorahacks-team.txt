Everything here was written during BUIDL CTC 2026 Fall, from an empty repository
on 11 September 2026: three Solidity contracts and their 49 tests, the autonomous
scout, the read API, the MCP server and the front end.

My earlier work keeps circling one question. How do you make a claim checkable by
someone who was not there?

- **Veritas Protocol**: an IP and settlement layer against AI plagiarism, built
  in Uniswap Hook Incubator 8 and a two-track winner of the 9th hookathon.
  Tinjau's risk-priced premium reuses that pattern.
- **VISTA**: pays a viewer 40% of the ad spend, but only when Proof of Attention
  shows they watched. 2nd place, Monad Blitz Hackathon Yogyakarta, April 2026.
  The same rule as the bounty contract here: money moves on evidence, never on a
  claim.
- **Ambang**: a research agent that emits replayable receipts over frozen market
  evidence, with hash chaining and Bitcoin timestamping.
- **LandProof**: immutable on-chain land-ownership records, patent under DJKI
  review.

Solidity and Foundry, TypeScript and Node.js, and Gemini, which reads payment
claims here and is kept outside the fact path.
