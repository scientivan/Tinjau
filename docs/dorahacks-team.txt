Everything in this submission was written during BUIDL CTC 2026 Fall, from an
empty repository on 11 September 2026: the three Solidity contracts and their 49
tests, the autonomous scout, the read API, the MCP server and the front end.

Why this problem found me. My earlier work keeps circling one question. How do
you make a claim checkable by someone who was not there?

- **Veritas Protocol**: a smart-contract layer for verifiable content ownership
  and AI-plagiarism protection, built in the Uniswap Hook Incubator and a
  two-track winner of its 9th international hookathon. Tinjau's risk-priced
  premium reuses that pattern, without its insurance reserve.
- **Ambang**: a research agent that runs deterministic strategies over frozen
  market evidence and emits replayable receipts with hash chaining and Bitcoin
  timestamping; 133 backend tests.
- **LandProof**: immutable land-ownership records with Indonesia's land agency
  as the regulatory intermediary; patent application under DJKI review.
- **Movo**: x402-protected USDC payments, tracking and settlement.

Tools already in hand for this build: Solidity and Foundry, TypeScript and
Node.js, and Google Gemini, which here reads payment claims inside review
documents and is deliberately kept outside the fact path.
