Everything in this submission was written during BUIDL CTC 2026 Fall, from an
empty repository on 11 September 2026: the three Solidity contracts and their 49
tests, the autonomous scout, the read API, the MCP server and the front end.

Why this problem found me. My earlier work keeps circling one question. How do
you make a claim checkable by someone who was not there?

- **Veritas Protocol**: a decentralised IP and settlement layer that protects
  creators from AI plagiarism through on-chain conditional licensing. Built as a
  mentee in Uniswap Hook Incubator 8, then a two-track winner of the 9th
  hookathon (Impermanent Loss & Yield Systems, Reactive Network). Tinjau's
  risk-priced premium reuses that pattern, without its insurance reserve.
- **VISTA** (Verified Instant Streaming Tokenized Attention): pays a viewer 40%
  of the ad spend, but only when Proof of Attention shows they actually watched.
  2nd place at the Monad Blitz Hackathon, Yogyakarta edition, April 2026. The
  same rule as Tinjau's bounty: the money moves only when evidence proves
  something, never on a claim.
- **Ambang**: a research agent that runs deterministic strategies over frozen
  market evidence and emits replayable receipts with hash chaining and Bitcoin
  timestamping. Built solo for RYO-CHAN Hackathon 2026; 133 backend tests.
- **LandProof**: immutable on-chain land-ownership records with Indonesia's land
  agency (BPN) as the regulatory intermediary. Patent application under DJKI
  review.
- **Movo**: cross-border stablecoin payments with x402-protected USDC, tracking
  and automatic settlement.
- **Luber**: an assistant that turns quantitative on-chain liquidity-risk signals
  into language a non-expert can act on, which is what this front end does with
  contract output.

Also a technical workshop speaker with the Sui Foundation, covering Move, ZK
Login and a milestone-based escrow.

Tools already in hand for this build: Solidity and Foundry, TypeScript and
Node.js, and Google Gemini, which here reads payment claims inside review
documents and is deliberately kept outside the fact path.
